How to strike it rich

Gold sits just above bedrock. Topsoil is worthless — strip it, then load the grey-gold gravel.
Pan stream gravel while standing in water, or fill the truck and dump it in the wash plant hopper.

Surviving the night

Skeletons attack after 20:30 and crumble at dawn. They go for you, your turrets, and the wash plant's sluice gold.
Driving over them works too. Buy turrets on your phone and place them around camp.
Build a Barracks and hire mercenaries with gold. Sleep at the Inn and the raid is fought as a dice battle — lose it and the horde wrecks your machines.
Command (slot 8): LMB / drag to select soldiers, double-click for all · RMB ground to move, RMB skeleton to attack · M move, Z patrol, X stand ground, H back to barracks.
